## Coursework & Evaluation Standard

The CS50 AI curriculum requires implementing core artificial intelligence algorithms from scratch across six key areas:
1. **Search** (Graph traversal, Breadth-First Search, Depth-First Search, A*, Minimax with Alpha-Beta Pruning)
2. **Knowledge** (Propositional logic, inference by resolution, model checking, knowledge bases)
3. **Uncertainty** (Probability theory, Markov models, Random Surfer PageRank, Bayesian Networks)
4. **Optimization** (Constraint Satisfaction Problems, Node/Arc consistency, AC-3, backtracking)
5. **Learning** (Supervised learning, K-Nearest Neighbors, Reinforcement learning, Q-learning, Deep Learning, CNNs)
6. **Language** (Context-Free Grammars, syntactic parsing, Attention mechanisms, BERT transformer models)

Every assignment is graded strictly by Harvard's automated grading harness (`check50`) and submitted directly through Harvard's academic version control system (`submit50`). A minimum passing score of **70%** is required on every single assignment to earn the certificate. In this repository, **100% of all test suites passed**.
