# Academic Honesty & Code Disclosure Statement

### Harvard University Division of Continuing Education & CS50

This repository complies strictly with Harvard University's Academic Honesty Policy for CS50's Introduction to Artificial Intelligence with Python:
[https://cs50.harvard.edu/ai/honesty/](https://cs50.harvard.edu/ai/honesty/)

---

## 1. Compliance Policy & Solution Secrecy

Harvard CS50 explicitly specifies:
> *"The following behaviors are considered dishonest: Providing or making available solutions to problem sets to individuals who take or may take the course in the future."*

To uphold this pedagogical principle and protect the integrity of future cohorts:
- **No restricted assignment solution files** (e.g., student implementations of problem set code) are published in this public repository.
- All assignment solution source files, test datasets, and distribution archives are excluded via `.gitignore` and maintained solely in private local workspaces.
- This repository serves exclusively as a **portfolio of achievement**, **educational architectural documentation**, **theoretical reference**, and **credential verification portal**.

---

## 2. Permitted Collaboration & Attribution
- All assignment tasks were executed, verified against the automated grading suite (`check50`), and submitted directly to Harvard's grading system (`submit50`) in full compliance with the course requirements.
- The theoretical algorithms, mathematical formulations, architectural diagrams, and documentation contained in this repository represent original explanatory work produced by **Bukya Naresh**.
- Problem descriptions, game specifications, and dataset schemas originate from Harvard CS50's course materials (instructors: David J. Malan and Brian Yu).

---

## 3. Policy for Current Students
If you are currently enrolled in Harvard CS50 AI:
- Please consult the official course documentation at [cs50.harvard.edu/ai](https://cs50.harvard.edu/ai/) and adhere strictly to Harvard's honor code.
- Do not solicit solution source code or assessment files.
- The mathematical summaries and algorithmic explanations provided in this repository are intended for conceptual study and portfolio showcase only.
