## Programme Overview

**CS50's Introduction to Artificial Intelligence with Python** is an intensive graduate-level introduction to the core principles and computational techniques that empower modern artificial intelligence. Offered by the Division of Continuing Education at **Harvard University** under instructors **David J. Malan** and **Brian Yu**, the course bridges foundational discrete mathematics and graph theory with contemporary statistical machine learning and deep transformer neural networks.

Through 12 comprehensive programming assignments, students design, implement, and analyze intelligent agents capable of logical deduction, probabilistic inference, heuristic optimization, computer vision pattern recognition, and natural language understanding.

## Academic Integrity Policy

In strict compliance with **Harvard University's Academic Honesty Policy**:
> *“Providing or making available solutions to problem sets to individuals who take or may take the course in the future is considered dishonest.”*
> — [Harvard CS50 Academic Honesty](https://cs50.harvard.edu/ai/honesty/)

- **No Student Solution Code is Uploaded**: All student implementation files (`*.py` problem set files), datasets, and pre-trained weights are kept strictly local and excluded via `.gitignore`.
- **Educational Knowledge Base**: This repository exclusively publishes independent conceptual descriptions, mathematical derivations, architecture diagrams, and academic verification references.
- For complete details, see [`DOCUMENTATION/academic-integrity.md`](DOCUMENTATION/academic-integrity.md).

## Future Development Roadmap

1. **Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL)**: Extend the Q-learning foundations from Nim to Deep Q-Networks (DQN) and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) for continuous control.
2. **Generative Vision & Diffusion**: Advance from classification CNNs to generative architectures (Variational Autoencoders, Diffusion Models).
3. **Large Language Model (LLM) Fine-Tuning**: Expand BERT attention visualization techniques to decoder-only causal models (Llama, Gemma) utilizing Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT/LoRA).
4. **Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)**: Unify classical search graph representations with inductive node embeddings and message-passing neural networks.
